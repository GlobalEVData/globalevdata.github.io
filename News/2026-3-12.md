# China E-Bus Data (2025): A New Dataset for E-Bus Simulation

> Posted on 12 March 2026 by Ding CHEN

We are pleased to present the China E-Bus Data (2025), a new dataset developed to support research on urban bus operations and electric bus system simulation in China. Covering 224 cities, the collection includes bus route and stop data, bus operation data, and road network data.

![Analytical framework](./imgs/2026-3-12-1.png)
> *Data-Available Cities Classified by Bus Network Length*

Together, these datasets provide a practical empirical foundation for the study of e-bus operations, charging infrastructure planning, and transport optimization. One-city sample files are available for download, and access to the full datasets used in our research can be requested through our Data Sharing Initiative: see [here](/datasets/index.md).
